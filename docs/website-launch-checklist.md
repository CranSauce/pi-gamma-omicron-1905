# Pi Gamma Omicron Website Launch Checklist

Prepared from the Vice President's proposed checklist and an audit of the current website source.

## Phase 1 implementation update

Completed in the first public-launch build phase:

- Dedicated About, Leadership, Membership, News & Events, and Contact routes.
- Explicit homepage actions for Learn Our History, Membership, and Start a Chapter.
- Public Charlotte, North Carolina — 2027 conference announcement with “Dates forthcoming.”
- Homepage upcoming-event feature and a public revival/news desk.
- Collegiate and alumni/graduate membership overviews, pillar-based expectations, and a clearly disclaimed interest process.
- Public inquiry routing for membership, chapter expansion, and historical research.
- Reduced, proportionally constrained footer crest.

Still awaiting leadership-supplied or leadership-approved material:

- Official Vision, Purpose, and public meaning-of-ΠΓΟ statements.
- National Organizer, Revival Committee, and chapter officer rosters.
- Leadership portraits and approved personal biographies.
- Formal membership qualifications and official expansion/reactivation requirements.
- General, media, and research inboxes plus official social links.
- Conference dates and registration details.

## Status key

- ✅ **Present** — clearly represented on the current site.
- 🟡 **Partial** — some content or functionality exists, but the requested section is incomplete or not prominent enough.
- ⬜ **Missing** — no public section or route currently covers the item.
- 🔒 **Leadership approval needed** — wording, status, names, images, or public visibility should be approved by fraternity leadership before publication.

## Recommended launch order

### Priority 1 — Public launch essentials

- Create a focused About page.
- Add clear homepage calls to action for History, Membership, and Start a Chapter.
- Create a Contact page with general, membership, expansion, and historical-research routing.
- Add the approved Charlotte 2027 conference announcement.
- Add a simple public News & Events route and homepage “Upcoming” module.
- Complete and approve leadership names, roles, biographies, and photographs.
- Confirm membership qualifications, pathways, expectations, and expansion language.
- Test the live members-only authentication and authorization flow.

### Priority 2 — Institutional depth

- Create Programs & Initiatives content from real, approved programs.
- Build the separate Heritage digital museum.
- Add a chapter directory and reactivation/interest-group statuses.
- Add a consent-based Gallery once approved media is collected.

- Public membership qualifications, expectations, pathways, and official process terminology.
- Confirmed active/reactivating chapter and interest-group statuses.
- Modern chapter expansion and reactivation requirements.
- Approved program and scholarship details.
- Charlotte 2027 announcement wording and eventual dates.
- Official social media links and contact-routing email addresses.
- Public/private policy for new members, member presentations, Mystery School material, directories, and galleries.
- Historical photos, original publications, significant-member biographies, dates, sources, and usage rights.
